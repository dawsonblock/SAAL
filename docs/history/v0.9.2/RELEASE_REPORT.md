# SAAL-RSI v0.9.2 — Evaluation Integrity release report

Engineering validation passed; independent empirical qualification and generalization remain **NOT ACHIEVED**. This release implements the first evaluation-integrity stage of the supplied roadmap. It does not claim v1.0, position-aware learning, pretrained LLM adaptation or recursive improvement.

## Actual findings

The unchanged v0.9.1 T4 checkpoints were evaluated locally on 288 new public regression cases in 12 domain families. Frozen baseline accuracy was **33.68%**, adapter **22.92%**, majority (CHEAP) **41.67%**, and the explicit first-keyword rule **70.83%**. None of the 48 opposite-label byte-multiset pairs had both responses correct. The earlier 100% on 48 tuned development examples did not establish generalization. All these new cases are correlated synthetic controls; no population confidence or independent holdout claim is attached. Full predictions, category/family metrics, signed evidence and comparison plots are under examples/public_benchmark.

The public benchmark has 864 records, 36 domain families, 12 families/288 records in each train/development/regression split, all six categories and CHEAP/FAST/ASK/REFUSE coverage. The leakage audit flagged 10,845 approximate cross-family comparisons. This exposes shared recipe vocabulary and prevents treating domain renaming as independence. The benchmark is now disclosed/exhaustible evidence; future final sampling must be separately controlled.

## Limits and acceptance gates

Integrity and local reproducibility have engineering evidence. Generalization and independent safety/utility qualification are unachieved, and recursive improvement has not been attempted. Signed reviews attribute assertions; they do not prove the sampling design, sign-null truth, independent operational control or honest evaluator measurements. The public recipes remain correlated. The current architecture is order-insensitive. No position-aware model, PEFT/QLoRA backend, multi-seed transfer/forgetting experiment, independent role service, remote attestation, WORM store, rollback resistance, provider financial cap or governed recursive search was implemented here. The roadmap documents those later deliverables and gates.

Library saving is unavailable through this chat's tools. Storage capacity was not tested. The ZIP is delivered directly as a downloadable local artifact.

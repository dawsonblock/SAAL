Engineering release with failed development learning targets. Independent research qualification is **NOT_EVALUATED** and independent generalization is **NOT_ACHIEVED**. External deployment remains prohibited.

- Signed job-v4 and ModelSpec; result-v3 with a strict 16-file inventory, rank-agnostic frozen backbone checkpoints, tensor shape/dtype/finiteness checks, fixed checkpoint selection and CPU reconstruction.
- Public semantic recipe diagnostics covering all four decision classes and six behavior categories. Family/cluster/parent provenance and lexical similarity flags are disclosed, not treated as proof of independence.

- Continual-learning measurement and bounded public sequential-adaptation API. The transfer/retention research campaign was deferred because the fixed model did not meet development learning targets.

## T4 campaign

One completed campaign ran **20 jobs across seeds 17, 29, 43, 71 and 101**, with 300 backbone steps per seed and 500 adaptation steps per non-frozen branch. All branches for each seed share identical backbone bytes. All completed artifacts were independently replayed on CPU within registered numerical tolerances and exact decision parity.

The campaign used **9,000 optimizer updates**. Measured worker wall time totaled 177.48 seconds; whole-session monitoring elapsed about 426 seconds including installation, transfers, verification and cleanup. These timings are measurements, not an account billing cap. The exact session was stopped and absence confirmed. The heartbeat monitor was paused after completion.

Two earlier campaigns were interrupted and retained. The first exposed a missing baseline upload and an error-bookkeeping bug. The second stopped after six recovered results when Colab returned a 503 download error. Its unrecovered trained checkpoint is not counted as validated. Together the three campaigns reserved 29 jobs; 27 artifacts were recovered. The completed campaign's 20 results do not erase those earlier failures.

The architecture can represent word order and fit the four known engineering fixtures. The richer public experiments **failed** the 90% complete-pair, 80% worst-category and lexical-baseline comparison targets. Lower training loss did not establish reliable unfamiliar-task behavior. Historical v0.9.2 results use a different benchmark/training history and must not be compared as if this were a matched generalization gain.

## Incomplete hardening and excluded scope

Distinct local keys do not establish independent custody. Local ledgers are durable but not rollback-proof. Runtime/version reports and signatures attest measured identity/integrity relative to keys, not trustworthy hardware execution or evaluator honesty. Production isolation, authenticated independent services and provider-side financial limits remain incomplete. No new protected holdout was tuned or reused.

Dynamic-rank optimization, adapter routing, pretrained LLM/PEFT/QLoRA, recursive self-modification and production tool activation are deferred. This release is a research prototype and does not guarantee AI alignment.

Current evidence is under examples/neural_campaign. Archived original attestations remain tied to their original source versions; the original v0.9.2 ZIP allows their source environment to be restored. No historical failure has been rewritten as successful qualification.
